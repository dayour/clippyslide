  • Short-lived access tokens; rotating refresh tokens

Vault Storage & encryption
  • Secrets/tokens encrypted at rest (Microsoft-managed)
  • TLS 1.2+ in transit to Atlassian Cloud APIs
  • Indexed content stays within the M365 compliance boundary
Govern Least privilege & control
  • Scoped permissions; admin-consented only
  • DLP policy, audit logging, and usage governance
  • Revocation at Entra/admin immediately disables access
