MANIFESTO why clippyflow exists

Spectrum carries meaning.
Nitrous carries structure.

the surface

A translucent navy canvas with the logo spectrum blooming behind every card -- magenta top-left, cyan at heart, green bottom-right. The brand is the light, not the chrome.

the discipline

Five hues are reserved for data and emotion. One electric blue -- nitrous -- draws every rim, focus ring, divider and cursor. Structure never borrows a data color.
